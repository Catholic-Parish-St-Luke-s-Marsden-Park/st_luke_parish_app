## Limits of this check (stated plainly)
- **Layout size:** Flutter's live test binding renders at its fixed 800×600 test surface, letterboxed on the phone. The screenshots confirm behaviour and content, not the exact phone-width layout.
  - Phone-width layout is covered by the widget tests' 360/390-wide sweeps of the new states.
  - Pre-existing phone-width overflows are RISK-013.
- **Mail CTA:** the Join tap wasn't exercised on the simulator, because the simulator has no Mail app. The encoded `mailto:` is covered exactly by widget and unit tests.
- **Human review:** the scripted check above came first. The user's hands-on review followed (see below).

## Hands-on review (user, 2026-10-08)
- **Setup:** the user ran the app on an iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator. Xcode 27's simulator window is DeviceHub (`open -a DeviceHub`); there's no `Simulator.app`.
- **Feedback:** "Ministries tab works. The card does look oversized."
- **Cause:** the grid cells had a fixed `childAspectRatio: 0.95`, so each card was about 210pt tall on a 440pt-wide phone, mostly empty space.
- **Fix:** the user chose grouped list rows over compact two-column cards. Each category is now one white card with a row per ministry: the name, a "members · day" line and a chevron.
  - Rows size to their content: about 64–80pt at 1.0x.
  - The text uses `TextTheme` styles of 13pt and up, and the subtitle uses `textSecondary` to meet 4.5:1 contrast.
  - Each row reads as one button to screen readers.
  - The list now passes a no-overflow sweep at 360x690 and 390x844 × 1.0x, 1.5x and 2.0x.
- The change went on this branch (PR #25) at the user's request.
- **Re-check:** the user re-ran the app on the iPhone 17 Pro Max with the compact rows (`37bde1c`) and approved the new layout: "the ministries page looks ok".
  - The same run logged push-token registration failures ("Connection refused" to `10.0.2.2:8080/tokens`). That's feature 07 config, not this PR: `BASE_URL` is the Android-emulator address, and an iOS simulator needs `http://localhost:8080/tokens` as a local, uncommitted edit. Ministries uses `API_BASE_URL` and is unaffected.
