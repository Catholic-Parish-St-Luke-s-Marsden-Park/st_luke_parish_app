## Feature Summary
Build the real content for the existing "Parish" bottom-nav tab (`lib/features/parish/presentation/screens/parish_screen.dart`), replacing its current generic `PlaceholderScreen` stub with a thin vertical slice of static, non-scraped parish content: a hero banner (headline + revised subtext, no eyebrow label), an "About St Luke's" section (existing planned body copy, but with no icon/logo row above it), and a new 3-part "Church Leadership" section (Universal Church / Diocese of Parramatta / Parish) replacing the reference site's separate "Parish Leadership" and "Diocese of Parramatta" cards. All leadership entries use locally bundled image assets and open their linked URL in an external browser on tap.

In addition, following a mockup review during this same BA round, this feature also covers the **shared app-level top bar** used across all 6 tabs: replacing the current bare `AppBar` title-only chrome (`lib/app/shell/app_shell.dart`) with the parish wordmark logo (bundled local asset) plus two pill action buttons — "Saint of the Day" (minimal hardcoded placeholder on tap) and "Ask Luke" (navigates to a new full-screen, hardcoded-response quick-reply assistant screen, no free-text input in this version).

The other 5 bottom-nav tabs' own body content (Masses, News, Ministries, Contact, Donate) remains explicitly out of scope and untouched by this feature; only the shared AppBar chrome they all inherit changes, and it changes identically for all of them.
